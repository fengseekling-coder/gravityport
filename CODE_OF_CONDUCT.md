# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

Project spaces must remain professional, constructive, and safe for participants with different backgrounds and experience levels.

Examples of acceptable behavior include:

- giving specific, actionable, and respectful feedback;
- focusing criticism on ideas, code, documentation, and observable behavior;
- respecting privacy, attribution, license terms, and project boundaries;
- acknowledging mistakes and correcting them.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized language, or personal attacks;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- deliberately sharing credentials, Tokens, private Prompts, user media, or identifying session data;
- sustained disruption, spam, or bad-faith use of project communication channels.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates these standards.

Report conduct concerns privately to [fengseekling@gmail.com](mailto:fengseekling@gmail.com) with the subject `[GravityPort Conduct]`. Include links or screenshots needed to understand the incident, but minimize unrelated personal data. Reports will be reviewed as privately as practical.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions, and other spaces where a participant is representing GravityPort.
